GranPhone replaces the phone's interface with a bigger, simpler one —
built for elderly people who find standard smartphones confusing.

Features:
- Home screen with large tiles: Phone, Contacts, SOS, Messages, Photos, Alarms, Flashlight
- Default dialer with an oversized incoming/outgoing call screen
- SOS button with a configurable emergency contact
- Pill reminders
- Optional kiosk mode (device owner) to lock the phone to essentials
- Immersive full screen, dark and light themes
- Works offline on old hardware (Android 6+, 1 GB RAM)
- No ads, no accounts, no telemetry

Forked from BaldPhone (Apache 2.0), with fixes and simplifications
for real low-end devices.
