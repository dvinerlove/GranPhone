Questions

Common Questions

Does BIG Launcher require buying a new phone? + −

No. It works on almost any Android phone or tablet — even older ones. If they already have an Android, install it today.

What about iPhone? My parent has an iPhone. + −

BIG Launcher is Android-only — Apple doesn't let any app replace the iPhone home screen. If the iPhone is too complicated for your parent, an affordable Android phone with BIG Launcher is the easy fix: big buttons, photo contacts and SOS, set up by you in minutes.

Does it work with WhatsApp, Facebook, and other apps? + −

Yes. Any app can be placed on the home screen as a large, labeled button. BIG Launcher doesn’t change what’s inside other apps — no app can do that — but it simplifies everything around them: getting to the app, getting back home, and never getting lost in between.

How does the SOS button work? + −

One tap on the red SOS button sends an SMS with their location to the family members you chose and calls you on speakerphone. A short countdown lets them cancel an accidental tap.

How is BIG Launcher different from Samsung Easy Mode? + −

Easy Mode just makes Samsung's own home screen a bit bigger. BIG Launcher is built for seniors from the ground up — photo contacts, an SOS button, hidden apps, locked settings and simple apps for calls and messages — and it runs on almost any Android, not just Samsung.

Can I customize what’s on the home screen? + −

Fully. Add multiple screens, place contacts with photos, app shortcuts, or widgets on any button. Change the number of buttons per screen, rearrange the layout, pick a color theme — make it exactly what they need and nothing more.

Is there a free version to try first? + −

Yes. All six BIG apps are free to download and try.

How much does BIG Launcher cost? + −

BIG Launcher is free to try. Choose a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan for BIG Launcher through Google Play. Prices vary by country and currency, and Google Play confirms the exact final price before purchase. See current prices for one app and BIG Apps Suite PREMIUM.

What if we get stuck? + −

There's a detailed user manual and step-by-step video guides — and you can always write to us. We reply ourselves, usually within a day.

Can I help my parent with their phone from a distance? + −

Yes, with our BIG Remote Help app. Your parent calls you with one tap. When they’re stuck, you see their screen and, with their permission, tap for them. You pair the phones once in person with a QR code; after that, you can help from anywhere. The free version includes 15 minutes of calls a month. See how BIG Remote Help works →
