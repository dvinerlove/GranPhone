## [](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WAI-AGE/additions.html)Introduction

The requirements listed in the table below are additional to the requirements provided by the various authors that match the WCAG 1.0 Checkpoints, and are considered by the respective authors to be part of good practice for ‘senior friendly’ Web sites.

These seven sets of recommendations or checklists have been analysed as they provided broad, and often extensive, recommendations for making Web sites ‘senior friendly’. As discussed in the [literature review](https://www.w3.org/TR/2008/WD-wai-age-literature-20080514/), they are either based on extensive research, considerable user observation, or both. Many other investigations have been reported in the literature review, however most of them either reported difficulties experienced by older users, or investigated and made recommendations for specific narrow aspects of web design/development issues, or addressed specific impairments experienced by older users.

### [](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WAI-AGE/additions.html)Key to symbol used in the tables

Key to symbol use in the table:

*   "Yes" means the author makes the recommendation
*   "-" means the author did not make the recommendation

Notes reflect qualifications about the author’s recommendation.
