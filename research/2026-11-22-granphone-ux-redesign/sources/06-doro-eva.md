It also offers quick and easy tutorials for first-time smartphone users."

## Pattern notes

- Verb-based IA (Call/View/Send/Add/Search/Set) instead of app-based IA — the user picks an action, the system picks the app. Distinctive vs. icon-grid launchers.
- Guided first-run setup covers accessibility prefs (hearing, vision, text size) — settings surfaced at onboarding, not buried.
