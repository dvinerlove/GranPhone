## Key verbatim quotes

- "Head into your Settings > Display" → "Tap on Easy Mode" → "Toggle on or off Easy Mode"
- "Once Easy Mode has been activated, you will notice the screen size & font will increase"
- Using Magnifier: "Swipe to the left your Home Screen" → "Tap on Magnifier" → "Use the Zoom function to zoom into text"
- Adding contacts: "Swipe across the Home Screen to view your Contacts page" → "Select Create Contact or Add existing contact"
- "If you would like to remove the contact from your Home Screen, tap on Delete"

## Context
Easy Mode changes the launcher to dedicated panels: left page = Magnifier tool, right page = Contacts page where favorite contacts can be pinned directly to the home screen (call-by-face pattern). Regular home screen remains in the middle. Enlarged screen items + fonts. No separate passcode or caretaker flow — it's just a user-facing display toggle.
