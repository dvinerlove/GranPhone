## Key verbatim quotes

1. "For most platforms, consider making touch targets at least 48 x 48 dp. A touch target of this size results in a physical size of about 9mm, regardless of screen size. The recommended target size for touchscreen elements is 7-10mm. It may be appropriate to use larger touch targets to accommodate a larger spectrum of users."
2. "an icon may appear to be 24 x 24 dp, but the padding surrounding it comprises the full 48 x 48 dp touch target"
3. "Note that on iOS, 44 x 44 pt is the recommended touch target."
4. "In most cases, touch targets separated by 8dp of space or more promote balanced information density and usability."
5. "Pointer targets ... Consider making pointer targets at least 44 x 44 dp."
6. Contrast table: "Large text (at 14 pt bold/18 pt regular and up) and graphics | 3:1 against the background"; "Small text | 4.5:1 against the background"
7. "Every added button, image, and line of text increases the complexity of a UI."

## Notes
- The key translation: 48dp ≈ 9mm physical, and Material itself cites a 7–10mm recommended range — for seniors, aim for the top of that range.
- 8dp minimum spacing between targets.
