## Key verbatim quotes (table rows; columns = 7 checklists)

1. Min text size: Holt 2000 "14 pt"; Age Light 2001 "12 to 14 pt"; NIH/NLM 2002 "12 to 14 pt"; Coyne & Nielsen "12 pt minimum"; Webcredible "12 pt minimum"; Zaphiris & Kurniawan "12 to 14 pt".
2. Typeface: near-universal "Sans serif" (Holt: "avoid fancy fonts").
3. Type weight: NIH/NLM "Use medium or bold weight"; Age Light "Avoid bold"; Holt "Bold for emphasis only".
4. "Avoid all capitals" recommended by Age Light, NIH/NLM ("use in headlines only"), Zaphiris.
5. Justification: "Left justified" (Age Light, NIH/NLM, Zaphiris); Holt "Not centred Not full".
6. Spacing: NIH/NLM "double space body text"; others "between lines & letters/lines".
7. Colour: NIH/NLM "Avoid blue & green in close proximity"; multiple "Dark on light is better than light on dark"; "avoid fluorescents".
8. Links/targets: NIH/NLM "Link text and its icon to increase target size"; Holt/Coyne "Larger size / 12pt+ & margin"; Webcredible "Add space between adjacent links to reduce chance of clicking the wrong one".
9. Mouse: NIH/NLM "Single clicks"; Zaphiris "No double clicks".
10. "Avoid pull-down & fly-out Menus": Holt "Yes", NIH/NLM "use pull-down menus sparingly", Zaphiris "Yes (particularly pull-down menus)".
11. "Use short pages": Holt + Age Light "Yes"; Zaphiris "to avoid scrolling". "Don't disable the 'Back' button": Age Light + Coyne "Yes".

## Notes
- Best accessible surrogate for the (currently CAPTCHA-blocked) NIA tip sheet: the NIH/NLM 2002 column is exactly the NIA/NLM "Making Your Website Senior Friendly" checklist. Consensus across all 7 sources: ≥12pt (12–14pt) body text, sans serif, left-justified, single clicks only, larger link targets, avoid pull-down/fly-out menus, short pages.
