## Key verbatim quotes

1. "Red and green are two colors that are especially heavily used in interface design because of their strong symbolic associations; red being a sign to warn or stop and green being the opposite."

2. "It was found that colorblindness varies from person to person, the need for solutions resulted in many creative work arounds, color blind people still understand color symbolism, charts and patterns are very difficult to understand, and current accessibility modifications alter the user experience."

3. "three implementations for design were outlined - Interfaces should retain meaningful colors, consistency is critical, and customization is ideal."

4. "Around 108 million web users are color blind" (thesis's figure; the more commonly cited global figure ~300M appears in secondary summaries — hedge the number)

## Notes
- Key takeaway for Q5: don't *remove* red/green semantics — colorblind users still understand the symbolism and rely on position/shape workarounds; keep color AND add redundant cues. Small n (4 interviews); treat as directional.
