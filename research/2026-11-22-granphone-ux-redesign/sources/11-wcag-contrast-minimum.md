## Key verbatim quotes

1. "The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, except for the following: ... Large-scale text and images of large-scale text have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1."
2. Large text definition: "18 point text or 14 point bold text is judged to be large enough to require a lower contrast ratio."
3. "14 and 18 point is roughly equivalent to 1.2 and 1.5 em or to 120% or 150% of the default size for body text"
4. Rationale: "The 4.5:1 ratio is used in this success criterion to account for the loss in contrast that results from moderately low visual acuity, congenital or acquired color deficiencies, or the loss of contrast sensitivity that typically accompanies aging."
5. "the user with 20/80 visual acuity would require contrast of about 7:1. This ratio is used in Success Criterion 1.4.6."
6. "20/40 is commonly reported as typical visual acuity of elders at roughly age 80."

## Notes
- The 4.5:1 AA ratio is explicitly calibrated for age-related contrast-sensitivity loss (~20/40 at age 80). For an elderly-first product, the AAA 7:1 (SC 1.4.6) should be the design target.
- "Large scale" = ≥18pt regular or ≥14pt bold (~120–150% of body text).
