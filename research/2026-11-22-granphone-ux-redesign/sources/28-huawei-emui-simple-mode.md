## Key verbatim quotes

- "Simple mode adopts a more concise layout for easy navigation, enlarges icons and fonts for the home screen, and maximizes the volume."
- "Go to Settings > System & updates > Simple mode and touch Enable."
- "Touch and hold any icon to drag it to the desired location or uninstall it if it is not a mandatory app."
- "Open Settings and touch Exit Simple mode."

## Context
EMUI Simple Mode = concise layout + enlarged icons/fonts + automatically maxed volume (unique to Huawei — addresses hearing, not just vision). Icon rearrangement/uninstall still allowed via long-press — keeps editability rather than locking the layout. Trivially reversible from Settings ("Exit Simple mode"). A Huawei sustainability article (not separately noted) claims ~8.8M monthly Simple Mode users in China.
